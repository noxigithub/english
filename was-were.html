Comic Stories: Past Continuous

The Interrupted Study

It was 11:00 PM last night. Alex was in his small dorm room. It was raining softly outside. He was preparing for a big history exam. He was wearing his favorite blue hoodie and headphones. The desk lamp was casting a soft light on his notes. He was reading about the Industrial Revolution. He didn't hear his phone buzz because he was concentrating so hard.

An Afternoon in the Park

It was a warm Saturday afternoon. The city park was full of people. Everyone was enjoying the good weather. While Sarah was painting on her easel near the trees, two children, Leo and Mia, were kicking a bright red football nearby. They were laughing loudly. A few meters away, an older man named Mr. Thompson was sitting on a wooden bench. He was reading a newspaper, completely relaxed.

The Culinary Adventure

It was Tuesday evening. Maria was in her kitchen. The lighting was warm and cozy. She was cooking a complicated recipe. She was whisking pancake batter in a big ceramic bowl. Suddenly, her father entered the room. He said something funny, and Maria was laughing when the photo was taken. Flour dust was floating in the air near her because she was mixing so fast.

The Misunderstanding

It was raining hard on Monday afternoon. The park was very wet. An older lady, Mrs. Davis, was walking her small white dog. She was wearing a bright yellow raincoat and holding a big blue umbrella. At the same time, a young man was jogging on the path. He wasn't wearing a raincoat; he was wearing grey running clothes. They were walking in opposite directions when they passed each other on the wet path.
