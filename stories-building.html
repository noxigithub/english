2. Weekend Trip Dilemma Travel & Reactions

Fluency Goal: Handle sudden changes of plans, adapt to weather events, and practice expressing emotions using get + adjective.
Key 'Get' Targets: get dark/windy get tired/cranky get nervous get to the park/hotel getting used to
