The Conditionals Stack

Welcome to the ultimate conditional review. Think of these as logical programming functions: IF a condition is met, THEN there is a result.

  • Zero Conditional (0): 100% Real. Facts, routines, server rules, universal truths.
    (If + Present, Present)
  • First Conditional (1): 50%-90% Real. Highly possible outcomes for future business plans.
    (If + Present, Will + Base Verb)
  • Second Conditional (2): 1%-10% Real. Hypothetical, imaginary, or "dream" scenarios.
    (If + Past, Would + Base Verb)

🔹 [0] If a customer clicks the ad, the tracking pixel fires.

🔹 [1] If the CEO approves the budget, we will hire more developers.

🔹 [2] If I had a million dollars, I would start my own tech agency.

Corporate Scenarios

Read the texts and answer aloud using the correct conditional structure.

Scenario 1: The New York Ad Agency

Sarah works at a top marketing agency in Manhattan, New York. Her team follows strict routines. For example, if they launch a campaign on Monday, they analyze the metrics on Friday. Right now, they are nervous about a new sneaker commercial. The rule is simple: if the commercial gets 100,000 views in 24 hours, the client will double their budget. Sarah dreams big. She often tells her team, "If we had Taylor Swift in our commercial, the sneakers would sell out in ten minutes!"
  • 1. What do they do if they launch a campaign on Monday? (Routine)
    Teacher: If they launch a campaign on Monday, they analyze the metrics on Friday. (Zero)
  • 2. What will happen if the commercial gets 100k views? (Future)
    Teacher: If it gets 100k views, the client will double their budget. (First)
  • 3. What would happen if they had Taylor Swift in the commercial? (Imaginary)
    Teacher: If they had Taylor Swift, the sneakers would sell out in ten minutes. (Second)

Scenario 2: The Los Angeles Social Media Manager

David manages social media for a tech startup in Los Angeles. He monitors engagement closely. If the engagement drops, he immediately changes the hashtags to reach new people. Currently, he is preparing a pitch for the board. If he finishes the content calendar tonight, he will present it tomorrow morning. However, David strongly disagrees with the company's name. He thinks to himself, "If I were the CEO, I would rebrand the whole company immediately to make it look more modern."
  • 1. What does David do if engagement drops?
    Teacher: If engagement drops, he changes the hashtags. (Zero)
  • 2. What will David do if he finishes the calendar tonight?
    Teacher: If he finishes it, he will present it tomorrow morning. (First)
  • 3. What would David do if he were the CEO?
    Teacher: If he were the CEO, he would rebrand the whole company. (Second)
