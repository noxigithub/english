Building Rhythm with Articles & 'GET'

🗣️ Teacher Step: Read the active blue line first. Have the student repeat it 3 times out loud focusing on connecting "get a" (/get-a/) and "to the" (/tu-tha/) smoothly.

To be honest... Actually... By the way... Well, you know... Anyway... At the end of the day...
Free Topic 1 of 14
Topic 1: Daily Errands & Coffee Run
"Tell me about a time you needed to get groceries or coffee in a rush. What happened, what did you get, and how was the traffic getting back home?"
💬 Teacher Role: Let the student speak freely for 2-3 minutes per prompt. Do NOT interrupt for minor grammar mistakes. Only take notes on missing articles (*a/the*) for a quick 2-minute review at the end.
