Understanding Prepositions: On, In, At

Master the usage of 'on', 'in', and 'at' for both place and time with clear explanations and practice exercises.

Prepositions of Place 📍

These prepositions help us indicate the exact location of something or someone.

On

Usage Description Examples
Surfaces When something is directly touching a surface. The book is on the table.
The picture is on the wall.
Lines/Roads/Streets For a specific street or road. My house is on Elm Street.
The bus travels on the highway.
Floors (in a building) To indicate a level in a building. My office is on the third floor.
The restaurant is on the ground floor.
Public Transport (large) Inside a public vehicle where you can stand and walk around. We are on the bus.
She is on the train/plane.

In

Usage Description Examples
Enclosed Spaces When something is inside a confined area with boundaries. The cat is in the box.
I am in the room.
Cities, Countries, Continents For large geographical areas. I live in London.
She was born in Brazil.
We are in Europe.
Bodies of Water When something is submerged or present in water. There are fish in the river.
He's swimming in the pool.
Cars/Taxis When you are inside a private vehicle. She is in her car.
He's in a taxi.
Books/Newspapers To refer to content within them. I read it in the newspaper.
The story is in the book.

At

Usage Description Examples
Specific Points/Locations For precise addresses or exact spots. I'll meet you at the corner.
She lives at 10 Downing Street.
Events/Public Places Referring to a general location or an activity taking place there. We were at the party.
I study at the university.
She's at the bus stop.
Buildings (general sense) To indicate being present in a building without specifying inside/outside. He's at the bank.
We're at the restaurant.
Home/Work/School Common fixed locations. I am at home.
He is at work.

Prepositions of Time ⏰

These prepositions help us indicate when something happens.

On

Usage Description Examples
Specific Days For days of the week. I have a meeting on Monday.
We leave on Friday.
Specific Dates For full dates. Her birthday is on July 15th.
The party is on Christmas Day.
Specific Parts of Days (with dates/days) When combined with a day or date. on a rainy afternoon,
on Monday morning.

In

Usage Description Examples
Months For specific months. My vacation is in August.
It gets cold in December.
Years For specific years. I was born in 1990.
The event happened in 2023.
Seasons For seasons of the year. We go skiing in winter.
Flowers bloom in spring.
Long Periods For decades, centuries, or the past/future. in the 1980s,
in the 21st century,
in the future.
Parts of the Day (general) For "morning," "afternoon," "evening." (Exception: at night) I like to read in the morning.
We work in the afternoon.

At

Usage Description Examples
Specific Times For exact clock times. The meeting starts at 9 AM.
I wake up at 7 o'clock.
Night This is a common exception. We often go out at night.
The stars shine at night.
Weekend (British English often) Used especially in British English. I'll see you at the weekend.
What are you doing at the weekend?
Holidays (without "Day") For general holiday periods. (But "on Christmas Day") We exchange gifts at Christmas.
They visit family at Easter.
