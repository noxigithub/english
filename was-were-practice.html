The Golden Rule of the Past! ✨

1. DID = Action (Acción) 🏃‍♂️

Usa DID cuando la pregunta tiene un verbo de acción (jugar, comer, correr, ir).

Fórmula: Did + Persona + Verbo (normal)

  • Did you play tennis?
  • Where did she go?
  • What did they eat?

2. WAS / WERE = State & Location 📍🎭

Usa WAS / WERE para preguntar por emociones, descripciones o lugares. ¡No hay otro verbo de acción!

Excepción: Si el otro verbo termina en -ing (jugando, comiendo), sí usas Was/Were.

WAS (I, He, She, It) WERE (You, We, They)
Was he happy? (Emoción) Were you at home? (Lugar)
Was the movie good? (Descripción) Were they tired? (Estado)
Where was she? (Lugar) What were you doing? (Verbo -ing)

🚨 WHERE vs WERE: La trampa mental 🧠

Suenan muy parecido, ¡pero son cosas totalmente distintas!

  • Where = DÓNDE (Es una pregunta).
  • Were = ESTABAS / ESTABAN (Es el verbo).

💡 Ejemplo de contraste:

Where were you? = ¿Dónde estabas tú?
