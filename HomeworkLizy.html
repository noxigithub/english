Interpreter Skills: Briefing Room

Review key expressions for accuracy, de-escalation, and terminology.

Checking for Accuracy

Double-checking numbers"Just to be absolutely sure, did you say fifteen (1-5) or fifty (5-0)?"
Pacing the speaker"Could you hold on for a second? I need to translate that first part."
Confirming context"Before I translate this to the client, are you implying that..."

De-escalation & Mediation

Neutral Stance"I am interpreting your exact words to the representative."
Validating Frustration"I understand you are upset. Let's see how we can resolve this."
Guiding to Solutions"I will ask them to explain the policy clearly for you."

Clarifying Terminology

Asking for Definitions"Could you explain what you mean by [term] in this context?"
Checking Layman's Terms"In other words, you mean that the system is..."
No Direct Translation"I am having trouble finding a direct translation for [word]. Can you describe what it does?"
