Interactive Stories: Used to & Agreement

Lee la historia con tu estudiante. Luego, practica los "Agreements" y finaliza con las preguntas abiertas para conversar.

❄️ Moving to Canada

PAST When I first moved to Canada, I suffered a lot. I wasn't used to the freezing weather.

PROCESS During the first winter, I slowly got used to wearing heavy coats and boots every day.

PRESENT CONTINUOUS Now, autumn is starting again. I am getting used to the shorter days and less sunlight.

PRESENT (STATE) However, I am used to the snow now.

Teacher says... (Student reacts)

1. I wasn't used to the freezing weather.
Neither was I
2. I got used to wearing heavy coats.
So did I
3. I am getting used to the shorter days.
So am I
4. I am not used to driving in the snow.
Neither am I
5. I am used to the snow now.
So am I

🗣️ Let's Talk! (Open Questions)

1. Have you ever moved to a new place? What did you have to get used to?
2. What is something about extreme weather that you would never get used to?

🥐 The New Bakery Job

PROCESS After a few weeks, I finally got used to the alarm ringing in the dark.

PRESENT CONTINUOUS Right now, I am getting used to going to bed at 8:00 PM to rest enough.

PRESENT (STATE) But to be honest, I am not used to having free time in the afternoons yet.

Teacher says... (Student reacts)

1. I couldn't get used to waking up at 4:00 AM.
Neither could I
2. I got used to the alarm ringing in the dark.
So did I
3. I am getting used to going to bed at 8:00 PM.
So am I
4. I haven't gotten used to the smell of bread yet.
Neither have I
5. I am not used to having free time in the afternoons.
Neither am I

🗣️ Let's Talk! (Open Questions)

1. What time are you used to waking up? Was it hard to get used to it?
2. If you had to work the night shift, how long would it take you to get used to sleeping during the day?

PRESENT CONTINUOUS Right now, I am getting used to driving in heavy traffic in the city center.

FUTURE I hope I will never go back to automatic. I love manual cars!

PRESENT (STATE) We are used to his barking when someone knocks on the door.

NEGATIVE But honestly, I haven't gotten used to all the dog hair on the sofa.
